Three simple steps

Make room for a better decision.

The goal isn't to stop you from buying things. It's to help you understand what you're choosing—and what you might be giving up.

01 / CLARIFY

Start with your need

Decide whether the purchase is essential, useful, or mostly an impulse.

02 / COMPARE

Look beyond the price tag

Consider total cost, expected use, affordability, payment terms, and returns.

03 / CHOOSE

Buy, wait, or skip

Review a suggestion with clear reasons. You stay in control and can decide differently.

Our principles

Helpful should not mean controlling.

Recommendation systems can shape what people want. This prototype puts transparency and user choice first.

01. No paid placement

Options are not ranked because a store pays us. This prototype has no ads or affiliate links.

02. No unnecessary data

We don't request bank logins or account access. Your entries are not sent to a server.

03. “Wait” is a valid result

The tool can recommend delaying or skipping a purchase instead of pushing a sale.

04. You make the final call

We explain the suggestion. You can disagree and choose what works for you.

The best purchase might be the one you decide not to make.

Buyer's Agent is a student prototype exploring how technology can support better choices without optimizing people into buying more.
